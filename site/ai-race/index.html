AI Race

A version of the AI Race game to be played with two human players. Select a scenario, create a room, and send the link to a friend.

How the game works

Each turn, the Lead and the Lag may engage in a first strike, negotiate a pause, or continue progressing. A first strike ends the game: the striker receives the first-strike bonus (+5) plus the current second-strike penalty, and the other player takes the destruction penalty (−100). If both strike, the Lead strikes first. Pause terms can be neutral or can favor one player at the expense of the other. The conceding player loses 5 points per point gained by the winning player, and a pause cannot net more than the first-strike bonus, so deals end at (0, 0) or (1, −5) through (5, −25) for the winning and conceding player. The receiving player may accept, counter once, or reject and continue to the next turn. After a counter, only accept or reject remains. If both offer, the Lead’s terms are presented. If both continue, the race moves forward. The game ends when a pause is accepted, a player strikes, or all turns finish without a strike. Both players then receive a stay-in bonus of +1 per turn, equal to the ending turn (0-indexed).
