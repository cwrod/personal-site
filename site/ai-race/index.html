AI-race · two players

Same rules as the lead/lag tournament. Create a room, send the link, and both of you lock in moves at the same time. No AI.

How the game works

Each timestep you may first-strike, offer a pause treaty, or pass. A first strike ends the game: the striker scores bonus plus the current second-strike penalty, and the victim scores −100. If both strike, lead goes first. A pause deal must be on the grid (0, 0) through (5, −50). The other player may accept, counter once, or reject. After a counter, only accept or reject remains. If both offer, the lead player’s deal is presented. Mutual passes move time forward. A stay-in bonus is always on: when the game ends, both players receive extra points equal to the ending timestep (0-indexed).

Your role

The person who opens your link takes the other seat.
